FAQ

What is aura?

A launchpad built to beat pump.fun on price. Coins launch on a bonding curve that charges 0.5% a trade instead of 1.25%, pays the creator on every trade, and charges snipers 99% in the first block.

Can I launch today?

Launches open with $AURA. The live market, the coin pages, trading through Jupiter and the aura check all work today.

Where do the coins on the board come from?

They're live pump.fun coins read from Jupiter's public token data. They aren't aura coins. Charts come from DEX Screener and trades go through Jupiter, signed in your own wallet.

What is the aura score?

Jupiter's organic score for the coin: how much of its trading looks like real people rather than bots. Higher is more aura.

How are the fee numbers worked out?

Each coin's last-hour volume from Jupiter times the fee: 1.25% for pump.fun's bonding curve, 0.5% for aura. Coins that have graduated may pay pump.fun a lower tier, so treat it as an estimate.
